About Us

Valente is an enviromentally friendly clothing company that strives to be inclusive to every age, body type, gender, etc.

Mission Statment

Our mission here at valente is to bring affordable, comfortable, and trendy clothing to everyone worldwide. Valente's mission is to also provide vegan, and eco-friendly clothing for individuals seeking stylish options. We leverage technology to connect with our customers while committing to research, utilizing organic materials, and embracing recycling practices.

How We Plan To Achieve This

  • Bring our Website to different Places around the world
  • Practice Safe Enviromentally Friendly Ways to Manufacture,Produce, and Market our Clothing
